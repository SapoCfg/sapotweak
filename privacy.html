SapoTweak Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SapoTweak collects, uses, and protects information related to the use and purchase of our software.

1. Information we collect

SapoTweak may collect or process limited information necessary to provide the product, activate licenses, provide support, and prevent abuse.

This may include:

  • Email address used for purchase or support
  • License key status and activation information
  • Basic device or activation identifiers needed for license validation
  • Support messages sent by the customer
  • Order, subscription, and payment status provided by our payment and billing providers

2. Payment information

Payments, subscriptions, taxes, invoices, and order processing are handled by third-party payment and billing providers. We do not directly store full credit card numbers or payment card details.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide access to SapoTweak
  • Validate and manage software licenses
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, license sharing, or unauthorized access
  • Improve the software and user experience
  • Comply with legal, tax, and platform requirements

4. What we do not collect

SapoTweak is not designed to collect passwords, browser history, private files, personal documents, game accounts, payment card numbers, or login credentials.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for payments, subscriptions, taxes, billing, fraud prevention, activation, and order delivery. These providers may process personal data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed to provide the product, manage licenses, process support requests, prevent abuse, and comply with legal or tax obligations.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no online service or software system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. User rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of your personal information. To request assistance, contact us using the email below.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: support@sapotweak.org
